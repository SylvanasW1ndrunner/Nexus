---
name: diagnose-with-evidence
description: Diagnose a problem from observable evidence and verify the proposed resolution.
---

Use this workflow when a task reports unexpected behavior, a failure, or an unclear result.

1. Gather the smallest set of observable evidence needed to reproduce or narrow the issue.
2. Distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses.
3. Identify the most direct corrective action, or explain what additional evidence is required.
4. Verify the resolution with an observable result and communicate the outcome clearly.

Use only tools that are available in the current task. Do not infer facts that the available evidence does not support.
